\begin{abstract}

k-kick 树相关理论表明，在插入和删除操作允许进行少量元素迁移的条件下，可以在保证常数时间查询的同时，获得更优的时间与空间权衡。本文在原有 k-kick 紧凑哈希表简化复现的基础上，进一步设计并实现了一种结合 hot tail cubby 与 SQLite 的冷热分层存储方案。该方案保留 Facility 分区、tail cubby 和局部路由等核心思想，将 tail cubby 改造为内存热缓存，用于存储近期插入的数据以及具有访问局部性的数据；同时将冷数据下沉至 SQLite 数据库中，以获得持久化能力。为验证该设计的实际效果，本文实现了三种对照方案，分别为原始多层级 cubby 哈希结构、纯 SQLite 存储结构，以及 hot tail cubby + SQLite 混合结构，并在相同的工作负载和评价指标下，从插入延迟、查询延迟、删除延迟和平均每键空间占用等方面进行实验比较。实验结果表明， hot tail cubby + SQLite 混合方案在保留数据库持久化能力的同时，能够利用内存热缓存降低热数据的访问成本，各项测试指标均优于纯 SQLite 方案。与原始多层级 cubby 结构相比，该方案的平均每键空间占用增加，但插入和删除延迟有所降低。

\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract*}

The theory of k-kick tree shows that, when a small amount of element migration is allowed during insertion and deletion, a better trade-off between time and space can be achieved while preserving constant-time query performance. Based on a simplified reproduction of the original k-kick compact hash table, this thesis further designs and implements a hot-cold hierarchical storage scheme that combines a hot tail cubby with SQLite. The proposed scheme preserves the core ideas of Facility partitioning, tail cubby storage, and local routing. Specifically, the tail cubby is transformed into an in-memory hot cache, which is used to store recently inserted data and data with access locality. Meanwhile, cold data is moved into an SQLite database to provide persistence. To evaluate the effectiveness of the proposed design, this thesis implements three comparative schemes: the original multi-tier cubby hash structure, a pure SQLite storage structure, and the hot tail cubby + SQLite hybrid structure. Under the same workloads and evaluation metrics, these schemes are compared in terms of insertion latency, query latency, deletion latency, and average space usage per key. The experimental results show that the proposed hot tail cubby + SQLite hybrid scheme preserves the persistence capability of the database while reducing the access cost of hot data through the in-memory hot cache, and all tested metrics are better than those of the pure SQLite scheme. Compared with the original multi-tier cubby structure, the proposed scheme has higher average space usage per key, but it achieves lower insertion and deletion latencies.

\end{abstract*}
